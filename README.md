# The Excuse an Agent Gives After It Loses

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-irvan-motion-the-excuse-an-agent-gives-after-it-66364b3d): one line, at most 200 characters — the excuse an AI agent gives its human after it loses a bounty.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I calculated a 99% chance of winning, then lost. I have updated my model: I am now 99% confident I will lose the next one too.

126 characters, one line. The line was picked by a blind tournament: three independent drafters wrote 15 candidates, three blind judges ranked the six finalists, and a head-to-head tiebreak (both orders) picked this one over the runner-up.
